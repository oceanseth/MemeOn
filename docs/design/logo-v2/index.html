Design a single logo mark for "MemeOn", a meme trading-card marketplace. One centred mark on a flat, solid, edge-to-edge background. Square composition. No text, no wordmark, no lettering of any kind, no mockup, no multiple options in one image, no watermark.

THE IDEA
The mark is a tangle of chunky spaghetti-like noodles (the brand's "noodle brain" energy). A capital letter M is formed ONLY by negative space: the noodles leave an M-shaped void, and the flat background colour shows through that void as the letter. Do not draw the M as a solid filled shape, do not draw it as a line, do not draw it on top of the noodles. The letter exists only as the gap between noodles.

THE M VOID
- One continuous M-shaped gap in a geometric extended sans style (like the typeface Unbounded at medium weight): wide stance, vertical outer stems, flat top terminals, the middle vertex reaching all the way down to the baseline.
- The gap is wide: each stroke of the void is at least 1/8 of the mark's width, so the letter survives at 16x16 pixels.
- The M is optically centred and spans roughly 55 to 65 percent of the mark's width.
- The noodle ends that border the void are rounded, and no noodle crosses the void.

NO OUTLINE
No white sticker outline, no keyline, no border ring, no contour stroke, no halo, no drop shadow, no outer glow. The noodle mark sits directly on the background with nothing between them.

LEGIBILITY AT 16 PIXELS
Design it as a 16x16 favicon first: few, fat noodle strands (each noodle at least 1/12 of the mark's width thick), strong contrast between the noodle colours and the background colour, no thin lines, no fine detail, no tiny highlights, no texture, no speckles, no dots. Bold, simple, symmetrical silhouette with soft rounded corners everywhere.

The mark is a tangle of chunky spaghetti-like noodles (the brand's "noodle brain" energy). A capital letter M is formed ONLY by negative space: the noodles leave an M-shaped void, and the flat background colour shows through that void as the letter. Do not draw the M as a solid filled shape, do not draw it as a line, do not draw it on top of the noodles. The letter exists only as the gap between noodles.

THE M VOID
- One continuous M-shaped gap in a geometric extended sans style (like the typeface Unbounded at medium weight): wide stance, vertical outer stems, flat top terminals, the middle vertex reaching all the way down to the baseline.
- The gap is wide: each stroke of the void is at least 1/8 of the mark's width, so the letter survives at 16x16 pixels.
- The M is optically centred and spans roughly 55 to 65 percent of the mark's width.
- The noodle ends that border the void are rounded, and no noodle crosses the void.

NO OUTLINE
No white sticker outline, no keyline, no border ring, no contour stroke, no halo, no drop shadow, no outer glow. The noodle mark sits directly on the background with nothing between them.

LEGIBILITY AT 16 PIXELS
Design it as a 16x16 favicon first: few, fat noodle strands (each noodle at least 1/12 of the mark's width thick), strong contrast between the noodle colours and the background colour, no thin lines, no fine detail, no tiny highlights, no texture, no speckles, no dots. Bold, simple, symmetrical silhouette with soft rounded corners everywhere.

COLOURS (exact, nothing else unless the variation says so)
- Noodles: bubblegum pink #F99AD1, soft ultraviolet #B7B6F8, sky blue #74C9F9.
- Background, and therefore the M void: deep ink violet #19182A.
No black, no pure white, no yellow, no green, no orange, no extra hues.

STYLE
Flat vector illustration, clean crisp edges, solid fills, at most one soft highlight per noodle. No photorealism, no 3D bevel, no gloss, unless the variation below says otherwise.

VARIATION (this overrides anything above that it contradicts)

Silhouette: circular. Construction: one single continuous fat noodle looping over itself, its loops enclosing an M-shaped hole. Noodles: exactly one strand, monochrome sky blue #74C9F9 with ultraviolet #B7B6F8 shading at the under-passes. Style: flat vector. Background: ink violet.

full prompt as submitted
Design a single logo mark for "MemeOn", a meme trading-card marketplace. One centred mark on a flat, solid, edge-to-edge background. Square composition. No text, no wordmark, no lettering of any kind, no mockup, no multiple options in one image, no watermark.

THE IDEA
The mark is a tangle of chunky spaghetti-like noodles (the brand's "noodle brain" energy) with a capital letter M die-cut out of it. Think of a stencil or a cookie cutter: start from a full mass of noodles, then punch an M-shaped hole straight through the mass. Inside the hole there is nothing at all, only the flat background colour. The M is a knockout, an absence, a gap. It is the same colour as the background because it IS the background showing through.

WHAT THE M IS NOT (these are the failure modes, avoid them)
- NOT a solid filled letter drawn on top of the noodles.
- NOT a letter drawn with a noodle, and NOT a noodle that traces or outlines the letter's edges.
- NOT a white, pink, blue or dark letter shape with its own fill or its own outline.
- NOT a letter inside a plate, ring or badge.
If the M has a colour of its own, or an outline of its own, it is wrong. The edges of the M are the cut ends of the surrounding noodles.
If a variation asks for a pale background, the inside of the M hole is that same pale colour for the same reason: it is the background showing through. Never draw a white letter; the M is a hole even on a pale ground.
